# Divine Display Ad Policy

**Last Updated: July 16, 2026**

## About this document

Divine Display's sponsor/ad rail exists to help mosques sustain their operations without turning the prayer-time display into a generic billboard. This Ad Policy explains what kinds of sponsors and announcements are accepted on displays run through this hosted instance of Divine Display, and why.

Divine Display is self-hosted, open-source software: mosques register their own account through this website and configure their own sponsors. **Each mosque administrator remains responsible for screening the sponsors they choose to display**, but every sponsor on this instance must meet the baseline standards below. If you operate your own separate instance, adapt this policy to reflect your own values and publish it alongside your own deployment.

This policy should be read together with our [Terms of Service](TERMS.md) (see [Your Content & Conduct](TERMS.md#your-content--conduct)) and [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).

## Our Commitment

As stated on this site: sponsorships exist so that community businesses can "increase [their] reach while supporting [a] local mosque through a halal-screened announcement network." Every sponsor and announcement shown on a Divine Display screen reflects on the mosque hosting it, and on the trust its congregants place in it. We hold sponsor content to three standards, in this order:

1. **Halal first.** Only community businesses that align with Islamic values are permitted to display announcements. No sponsor is accepted unless the business, product, or service being promoted is halal and free of clear ethical concern. When in doubt, we decline.
2. **Community benefit.** Local businesses should support their spiritual home while growing their reach within the community — advertising exists to strengthen the local Muslim community, not to extract attention or money from congregants for its own sake.
3. **Dignity of the space.** The display's primary purpose is always prayer times, Adhan, and Iqamah. Sponsor content is a secondary, unobtrusive presence — never a distraction from worship.

## Halal Screening

We do not accept sponsors or announcements for businesses whose primary activity involves any of the following:

- **Riba (interest-based finance)** — conventional/interest-bearing loans, credit cards, or lending products;
- **Alcohol, cannabis (recreational), tobacco, or vaping products**;
- **Gambling, lottery, betting, or games of chance**;
- **Non-halal food or meat**, or restaurants that do not serve halal-certified or halal-sourced meat;
- **Adult content, dating services, or anything promoting behavior contrary to Islamic values**;
- **Multi-level marketing (MLM) or other schemes we reasonably believe to be predatory or deceptive**;
- Any product, service, or message that is **haram**, promotes **shirk**, or is otherwise inconsistent with Islamic teachings.

If a business operates in a mixed industry (e.g., a grocery store that also sells alcohol), only the halal portion of its offering may be advertised, and the sponsorship should be evaluated on a case-by-case basis, ideally in consultation with the mosque's own religious leadership when unclear.

## Community Benefit Priority

Beyond halal screening, we prioritize sponsors and announcements that:

- Are owned or operated by members of the local community, or serve the local Muslim community directly (halal restaurants, Islamic schools, clinics, real estate agents, community events, etc.);
- Provide a genuine service or product at a fair price, with truthful, non-exaggerated claims;
- Support the mosque's mission — financially, through in-kind donations, or through community programming — rather than simply purchasing screen time.

Local community businesses and non-profits may be offered reduced-rate or complimentary ad-rail slots at a mosque's discretion, consistent with our belief that this display should circulate benefit within the community, not just revenue.

We do **not** accept:

- National or global advertising campaigns unrelated to the local community, run purely for reach;
- Political campaign advertising or partisan messaging;
- Announcements soliciting donations on behalf of organizations that have not been vetted for legitimacy.

## Content Standards

All sponsor and announcement content submitted for display must:

- Be **truthful and not misleading** — no exaggerated claims, fake urgency ("today only!" when it isn't), or manipulated pricing;
- Be **appropriate for a place of worship viewed by all ages** — no content that is violent, sexually suggestive, or otherwise unsuitable for a family/mosque setting;
- Be **legible and accessible** at a glance — the display is often viewed briefly, from a distance, by people arriving for prayer;
- Include only **imagery and language consistent with modesty standards** (e.g., no images that would be considered immodest under Islamic guidelines);
- Not contain **flashing, strobing, or attention-hijacking animation** that could distract from the prayer countdown or Adhan/Iqamah alerts.

## How Sponsors Are Displayed

Sponsor content appears only in the optional ad rail and promo rotation, and is **never shown during the critical-signal window** (the full-screen Adhan/Iqamah alert) — prayer times always take priority over any sponsor content. Placement follows each mosque's own configuration:

- **Fixed slots** — a specific sponsor is always shown in a given ad-rail position.
- **Dynamic slots** — sponsors rotate on a timer, weighted by the rotation weight configured for each sponsor.
- **Promo rail** — occasional, time-boxed sponsor promotions that rotate on a set interval, without interrupting the core prayer-time display.

Viewers can disable the sponsor rail entirely from their local display settings; this preference is stored only on the display device (see the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md#cookies-and-local-storage)) and does not affect other displays.

## Review and Approval Process

Every sponsor and announcement submitted for display on this instance is expected to be reviewed before it goes live:

1. The mosque administrator (or a designated committee member) reviews the sponsor's business, product, and proposed content against this policy.
2. Where the halal status of a product or business is unclear, we encourage consulting the mosque's imam or religious committee before approving.
3. Approved sponsors are added to the configuration; content can be edited or removed by an administrator at any time through the dashboard.

## Removal and Enforcement

We reserve the right to remove any sponsor or announcement from a display on this instance at any time, without notice, if it:

- Is found to violate this Ad Policy after being approved (e.g., a business changes its offering to something non-halal);
- Receives credible community complaints about its accuracy, appropriateness, or halal status;
- No longer reflects a genuine, ongoing relationship with the mosque or community.

There are no refunds implied by this policy for removed sponsorships; specific commercial terms (pricing, refunds, contract length) are handled separately between a mosque and its sponsor, outside of this document.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Ad Policy from time to time to reflect new guidance or changes in how the ad rail works. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date above.
